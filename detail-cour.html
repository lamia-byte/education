Description

This course is built for students who understand English but freeze when it's time to speak. Instead of more grammar drills, you'll practice the way fluent speakers actually learn: through repetition, real conversations and guided feedback. Every module ends with a speaking challenge you record yourself, so you can track your progress from your very first sentence to full, natural conversations.

By the end, you'll be able to hold conversations at work, while travelling, and in everyday situations, without translating in your head first.
